DriveAssistPrivacy

Effective September 12, 2026

Privacy by architecture.

DriveAssist is designed to analyze camera frames and location data on your device.

Camera

Camera frames are processed in the browser for object and lane estimates. DriveAssist does not upload, record, or store the video stream.

Location

While driving mode is active, location is used to calculate and display speed. Coordinates are not stored by DriveAssist or transmitted to an application server.

Settings and alerts

Your selected speed limit, warning preferences, and recent in-session alerts remain on your device. Recent alerts are cleared when the page reloads; preferences use browser local storage.

Third-party model delivery

TensorFlow.js and COCO-SSD assets are downloaded from jsDelivr and Google-hosted model storage. Those providers may receive standard request metadata such as IP address and user agent. Previously downloaded assets may be cached for offline use.

Permissions

Camera, location, and notifications are controlled through browser permissions. Stop driving mode or revoke the permissions in browser settings at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/naveenalavilli/driveassist.
