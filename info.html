A second set of eyes.
..but not a replacement for yours.

DriveAssist provides local visual and audio cues for selected road objects, speed, and lane position. Camera analysis is experimental and can miss or misidentify hazards.

Aa

Experimental sign reading

Reads selected U.S. English text signs: speed limit, stop, yield, do not enter, wrong way, school, pedestrian crossing, and road work / closure. Repeated reads adjust warning urgency. A lower speed-limit estimate applies for 30 seconds; your selected threshold remains the maximum. Symbol-only signs and conditional limits are unsupported.

⌖

Road objects

Recognizes people, vehicles, traffic lights, and stop signs using COCO-SSD. It does not recognize every road sign.

◎

Relative proximity

Uses bounding-box size to label objects far, mid-range, or near. It does not claim an uncalibrated distance in meters.

⇥

Lane guidance

Estimates bright lane markings in good visibility. Weather, road quality, curves, and camera placement reduce reliability.

Use responsibly

Experimental software—not a vehicle safety system.

Mount your phone securely and set up DriveAssist only while parked. Never interact with the screen while moving. Obey posted signs and applicable laws. Do not rely on this app to prevent collisions, determine safe speed, or keep a vehicle in its lane.
