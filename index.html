Before your first drive

DriveAssist is experimental. It can miss hazards or misread signs. Never rely on it to prevent a collision or steer your vehicle.

Securely mount your phone with the rear camera facing the road. Camera access enables local analysis; location enables speed estimates. Neither is uploaded. Keep this screen open during use.

Sign reading supports selected U.S. English text signs. Repeated speed-limit reads may lower your alert threshold for 30 seconds; they never raise your selected threshold. Check actual signs. Symbol-only and conditional limits are not supported.

Motion access enables local rotation and strong-movement cues. It cannot measure driving speed; speed alerts pause without GPS. You can decline permission and keep using camera assistance.
